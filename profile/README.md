### The moment is yours.

The highlight you watch again. The race photo you keep. The game you understand better.

We use computer vision to turn sport into data, then data into products that help people **play, improve, remember and connect**. Our own engineers build the technology, in-house.

<br>

## The products

<sub>01 / BAM · PADEL</sub>

### Every player's game seen, shared and celebrated.

From the court to the replay. BAM brings together full games and highlights, live streaming for friends and fans, VAR replay, leagues, round robins, matchmaking and club leaderboards.

**Analytics — coming soon.** Heat maps, shot types and errors.

[Explore BAM through our website ↗](https://fpslabs.ai)

---

<sub>02 / SNAPRUN · RACE PHOTOS · COMING SOON</sub>

### Cross the line. The photo is already yours.

Race-day capture for marathons. Cameras along the course photograph runners, with the goal of delivering their pictures before the medal does.

**SnapRun is a working name.** The product is in development.

[Talk to the lab about race-day capture ↗](mailto:support@fpslabs.ai)

## One lab. Different applications.

**FPS means Frames Per Second.** We capture sport frame by frame and build the pipeline that connects movement to a result.

| Capture | Understand | Deliver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| See the sport, frame by frame. | Find the person. Frame the moment. | Bring the highlight, photo or insight to the person it belongs to. |

FPS Labs is the company and the technology behind the products. This is our public home for the lab's introduction, documentation and future product updates.
